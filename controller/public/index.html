Initial setup

  1. Fill in Settings below and press Save Settings.
  2. Open OBS and enable its WebSocket server.
  3. Press LOGIN TIKTOK and sign in once.
  4. Start your TikTok LIVE and add the products you want to pin.
  5. Press Refresh Connections, then create your mappings.
